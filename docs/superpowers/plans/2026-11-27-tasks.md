# Tasks: a calendar view, and documents on a task

> **Review log:** written 2026-11-27 from the feature audit (docs/feature-audit-2026-11-27.md, items 5 and 36), against
> server `main` fa7fc70, web `main` 8c16f13, mobile `main` 3946509. Four-lens review on this plan before Task 1
> (folded in; see "Review changes" at the end), and again on the branch before merge.

**Goal:** (5) /tasks gets a third layout, Calendar, next to List and Board: a month grid placing each task on its due
day, honouring the View, Area and Report filters, a task opening in the existing side peek (`?task=`). (36) A task can
carry documents (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, plain text, and JPG/PNG as files), not only Before/After brand
photos, stored safely and downloaded, never rendered.

**Architecture:** The content calendar's month grid (web `MonthView` in components/workspace/content-panels.tsx) is
made generic over the item it shows and moved to its own file; the content calendar keeps its fetch, filters, legend
and New post around it, and the task calendar is a second thin wrapper. The server's task list gets an optional due-day
range on the same `listSql` (so visibility, client rules and the views come for free). Documents get their own table,
`TaskFile`, with their own task-scoped upload slot, sniffing check and download URL, apart from `MediaUpload`, so no
document can ever reach the story picker, the photo library or the story renderer.

**Tech Stack:** as Phase 1 (Next.js routes, Prisma 6, zod, vitest; `npm run sync:shared`). No new npm dependency.

**Estimate:** 4 dev days (T1 0.5, T2 1.25, T3 0.75, T4 1.5), plus the branch review.

## Decisions (my calls; the owner may override any)

1. **Calendar is a layout, not a view.** `TaskLayout` becomes `list | board | calendar`, remembered with the view in
   `tasks.view.v1` (readSaved accepts "calendar"; an old saved value still reads). The View select shows for List and
   Calendar (the board keeps its fixed open + done columns). Area tabs and the Performance Report filter apply as on the
   list. A `?view=` link (Today's group headers) opens the List, since approvals are often undated.
2. **Tasks sit on their due day as the brand's calendar day** (`dueOn`, YYYY-MM-DD), read as a string, never through
   `new Date()`, so no viewer's time zone moves a task to the day before.
3. **Undated tasks are not on the calendar.** A line under the grid says "Tasks without a due day are in the List."
4. **Done and cancelled:** the calendar shows what the View lists. "All open" shows open tasks; "Done (30 days)" shows
   tasks done in the last 30 days whose due day is in the month. Cancelled tasks never show (no view lists them).
5. **No drag to reschedule, no week view** in this slice (the content calendar has neither). A card opens the peek; the
   due day is changed there.
6. **One read per month shown, up to 200 tasks.** GET tasks takes `dueFrom` and `dueTo` (YYYY-MM-DD, both or neither,
   from ≤ to, at most 62 days apart); with them the page is 200, not 50. Past 200 the calendar says "Showing the first
   200 tasks of this month"; the List pages through all.
7. **Documents are a new table, `TaskFile`, not a new `MediaUpload` kind.** `MediaUpload` feeds the story picker
   (`listStorySources`, `resolveSource` "upload", which treats any non-video as a photo) and its purge; a document there
   would surface as a story photo with a signed URL to anyone with content.create, outside task visibility. A separate
   table keeps every existing reader untouched.
8. **Allowed files** (shared `TASK_FILE_TYPES`: type, extension, label only; the byte rules stay on the server), each
   checked by its bytes, never by name or browser claim: PDF (`%PDF-` at offset 0); DOCX, XLSX, PPTX (a ZIP with one
   end-of-directory record, no ZIP64, whose central directory lists `[Content_Types].xml` and `word/document.xml`,
   `xl/workbook.xml` or `ppt/presentation.xml` respectively; refused with `vbaProject.bin` or `activeX/` anywhere, or a
   `[Content_Types].xml` naming `macroEnabled`); CSV and TXT (valid UTF-8, no NUL byte); JPG and PNG (the existing
   `imageType`). The type found must be the type announced, or the file is refused. Not allowed: legacy DOC/XLS/PPT
   (OLE, macros), HTML, SVG, archives, executables, anything else.
9. **Limits:** 25 MB per file; 20 files per task (pending and checking ones count); 30 upload slots an hour per person
   (`task-file:<userId>`); a slot's PUT URL lives 10 minutes and the slot can be verified for an hour; at most 2
   verifies at once per server process (429 past that); an unfinished upload is removed by the daily sweep after a day.
10. **Who may add or remove a document:** staff who may edit the task's checklist: `managesTask`, or the task's owner,
    with no extra key (photos need content.create because they join the brand's media; documents don't).
    `lockTaskForEdit(tx, v, perms, taskId)`. **A client reviewer is refused (403) before that**, even as the task's
    owner (a client can be made owner of a task they see, `canBeAssigned`): files from outside never reach the team
    through a task.
11. **Who may download:** anyone who sees the task (visibleTaskSql), as with photos and the checklist. **That includes
    a client reviewer on a client-visible task** (they review it, or its project is shared). There is no per-file
    "internal" flag in this slice; whenever `task.clientVisible`, the files list says "A client reviewer can see this
    task and download these files." Files added while the task was internal become downloadable to the client once it
    turns client-visible. Ceiling: an internal flag mirroring comments.
12. **Downloads are always attachments.** The signed GET carries `ResponseContentDisposition: attachment` with
    `downloadName` (the display name's stem reduced to `[A-Za-z0-9._-]`, leading `._-` stripped, at most 80
    characters, then the verified type's extension; `file.<ext>` when nothing is left; tested to always pass
    `signedMediaUrl`'s name check, so one name can never 500 the task view) and `ResponseContentType` the verified
    type (`signedMediaUrl` gains an optional content type; existing callers unchanged). The object itself is stored
    with the verified type. The display name (trimmed, control characters removed, at most 120) is shown as React text
    only. A document is never put in an `<img>`, `<iframe>` or `<object>`. URLs live an hour, as the photos' do; the
    peek re-reads the task on every change, which renews them.
13. **Storage keys:** `<workspaceId>/<brandId>/task-files/uploads/<uuid>.<ext>` while unchecked, then
    `<workspaceId>/<brandId>/task-files/<uuid>.<ext>` (same uuid), built as uploads.ts does: `mediaKey(scope, file)`
    validates the tenant segments and the server-made name, then the folder is spliced in. Nothing from the client
    reaches a key. The checked bytes are written to the final key from the one read that was checked, so swapping the
    object after the check changes nothing.
14. **Removing a document** deletes the row (only a VERIFIED one, scoped to task, workspace and brand), then the object
    (best effort). A failed object delete leaves an orphan object, never a dangling row. Tasks are never deleted, so
    the FK's cascade only matters for hand-run SQL.
15. **Before/After photos are unchanged:** same flow (stories upload, `TaskAttachment`, role BEFORE/AFTER/OTHER), same
    rules. The panel gets a "Files" list under the photos.
16. **The phone** gets nothing new to show (it has no task screen); only the shared copy is synced.
17. **A refused file is deleted, not kept as REJECTED**, and verify answers 400 with the reason (the stories route's
    200 + REJECTED body exists for the phone; the web reads errors from status codes). The verify steps are not shared
    with `verifyUpload` (its kinds, rejected-row bookkeeping and video probing differ); the storage helpers
    (`presignedPut`, `getMediaBytes`, `putMedia`, `deleteMedia`) are. On the web, the slot-PUT step is one helper used
    by screenshots and files.
18. **The browser's file type is not trusted:** the web announces the type from the file's extension. CSV and TXT
    can't be told apart by bytes: UTF-8 text is accepted as either. An ANSI CSV (Excel's default) is refused with
    "Save it as CSV UTF-8 and try again."

## Facts from the code

- `listSql` (server/tasks/list.ts) builds every view on `visibleTaskSql`; open views sort on
  `DUE = COALESCE(t."dueOn", 'infinity')`, the expression the `task_open_due` partial index has. Done sorts by
  `completedAt` on `task_done`. `PAGE = 50`, `LIMIT PAGE + 1`, LIMIT is a bound value.
- `toTaskDtos` reads people and last submitters once per page.
- `MonthView` (content-panels.tsx 880–1128) holds the grid (`monthGrid`), cell sizing (`dayCards`), arrow keys
  (`gridStep`), the day popover (`DayPopover`, `popoverSide`), the narrow agenda (`AGENDA_BELOW`), and post specifics:
  the content fetch, `filterItems`, `PostCard`, `DayPosts`, `postCount`, `dayCellLabel` ("post"/"posts"), the narrow
  dots' tone, "New post", the legend. The narrow styles test `@container/cal`. The weeks share the height through
  `flex-1` on wrap, table and weeks box.
- A client can own a task (`canBeAssigned`, server/tasks/links.ts); `lockTaskForEdit` without `ownerNeeds` lets any
  owner in.
- `getTask` (server/tasks/service.ts) returns attachments with 1-hour signed URLs; the web peek re-reads on any change.
- `signedMediaUrl(key, s, downloadAs)` throws on any name outside `^[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9._-]*$`; `mediaKey` refuses a
  `/` in the name.
- `presignedPut` signs content-type and content-length. `getMediaBytes(key, max)` stops reading past `max` but copies
  the bytes twice.
- `TX` transactions time out after 20 s.
- The daily sweep's isolated steps (server/jobs/runner.ts) take a `Date`.

## Ceilings

- Verify buffers the whole file (≤ 25 MB, one copy) in memory, at most 2 at once per process. Upgrade: ranged GETs for
  the head and the ZIP directory, and a server-side copy.
- The ZIP check doesn't read `_rels` (remote templates) or embedded OLE objects (`embeddings/*.bin`); files are only
  downloaded, never opened by us, and the forced .docx/.xlsx/.pptx extension keeps Office's macro-free handling.
- CSV formula injection (`=HYPERLINK(...)`) reaches whoever opens a CSV in a spreadsheet; it is the uploader's file.
- Text files are never previewed: an HTML or SVG text passes as TXT/CSV and is safe only because it always downloads.
- The calendar's 200 rows carry full task DTOs (descriptions too), polled every minute as the list is.
- Download links die after an hour in a peek left open with no change; reopening the task renews them.
- No per-file internal flag (Decision 11).
