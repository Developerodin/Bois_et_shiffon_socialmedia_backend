# Overview gaps: the next 7 days as a list, and a compact performance summary

> **Review log:** written 2026-11-27 from feature-audit items 2 and 3 (docs/feature-audit-2026-11-27.md, "Overview and
> shell"), against server `main` fa7fc70 and web `main` 8c16f13. Four-lens review on this plan before Task 1 (below,
> "Review changes"), and again on the branch before merge.

**Goal:** The Overview lists what is coming up in the next 7 days (posts, website releases, project milestones), each
row opening its record, and shows a few key performance numbers (Meta ad spend and ROAS, Shopify sales) that each open
the Growth reports tab they come from. Nothing pretends to be data: a source that isn't connected or turned on says so.

**Architecture:** One new read route on the server (`GET brands/:b/upcoming`, tasks.read) for the website tasks and
milestones due in the brand's next 7 days, built on the task visibility SQL (`visibleTaskSql`), `brandLocalDay`,
`toTaskDtos` and the project visibility rule (`projectWhere`). Posts come from the content list the Overview already
polls (no new request). The performance summary is web only: it reuses `GET data-sources`, `GET ads/summary` and
`GET sales/summary` with the Performance page's default range. No migration, no new permission, no new flag.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js routes, Prisma 6, zod, vitest (unit + `test:db`); web components; `npm run sync:shared`.

**Estimate:** 2 dev days (T1 0.5, T2 0.5, T3 0.75, review and fixes 0.25).

## Decisions (owner may override)

1. **Where it shows:** both new sections sit in the Overview's Home panel ("Today & agents"), under Today and above
   Agents: **Next 7 days**, then **Performance**. Home is on the board in every preset but Classic; the Analytics panel
   keeps the SEO/GEO scores (its gate is analytics.read, which says nothing about ads or sales).
2. **Next 7 days replaces Today's "Posts in the next 7 days" count row.** The list says the same and more; keeping both
   would show the posts twice. Overdue and Blocked rows stay in Today.
3. **Window:** posts use the existing `isThisWeek` (scheduled from now to now + 7 × 24 h, not published), so the list
   matches the calendar's "Next 7 days" filter it links to. Tasks and milestones are dated by day, so they use the
   brand's local today through today + 6 (`brandLocalDay`, as Today's tasks do). Overdue tasks are left to Today's
   My tasks; overdue milestones are left out (the project page shows them).
4. **Website release** = an open task (To do, In progress, In review) with `WEBSITE` among its areas and `dueOn` in the
   window, visible to the viewer (`visibleTaskSql`: a client sees only shared ones; a staff member without tasks.all
   sees only their own). Opens in the Overview's task peek.
5. **Milestone** = a `WorkProjectMilestone` with no `doneAt`, `dueOn` in the window, in a project that is ACTIVE and the
   viewer may see (`projectWhere`: a client only shared projects). Shows the project name; opens `/projects/:id`.
6. **Caps:** at most 10 tasks and 10 milestones from the server, with total counts; posts capped at 10 in the browser.
   The section shows at most 8 rows in date order and says "N more" with links (calendar, Website tasks, Projects).
7. **Who sees what:** posts for everyone who sees Today now (unchanged); tasks and milestones only with the `tasks`
   flag and tasks.read (the route checks tasks.read again). With nothing in the window: "Nothing scheduled in the next
   7 days." With a part that failed to load: the rows that did load plus a LoadFailed line with Retry.
8. **Performance range:** the 28 days ending yesterday in the brand's zone (`presetEnd(zoneToday(timeZone))`,
   `presetRange(28, end)`), the Performance page's default for Ads and Sales. Each number links to
   `/performance?tab=ads|sales&from&to` with that range, so the tab opens on the same figures.
9. **Ads numbers:** shown only with the `ads` flag and ads.read. Spend and ROAS per currency (never added across
   currencies), labelled "Meta Ads · in the ad account's currency; ROAS as Meta reports it" as on the Ads tab. No
   META_ADS source: "No ad account connected" and a link to the tab (where an owner picks one). A source with no rows
   in the range: "No ad spend in these 28 days." No zeros are drawn for a source that has nothing.
10. **Sales numbers:** shown only with the `sales` flag and crm.read. Net sales excl. VAT (the Sales tab's headline,
    with its "Excl. VAT from …" / orders-left-out notes shortened to one line) and orders, per currency. No
    SHOPIFY_ORDERS source: "Orders aren't turned on" and a link to the tab. No orders: "No orders in these 28 days."
    `netExVat` null shows "—" with the reason, never 0.
11. **Customer follow-ups are left out.** There is no follow-up data (feature 26 not started; no stored inbox; Quotes
    sit behind the `crm` flag, off in production, and their reminders are not a follow-up queue). The brief says omit
    when there is no data; nothing is drawn for it, and the plan names it as a ceiling.
12. **Shop revenue ÷ ad spend is not repeated** on the Overview: it needs both keys and a long caveat; it stays on the
    Ads tab. The Overview shows the two headline figures the audit item names.
13. **No polling** for the performance numbers: read once when Home mounts and again on Retry (they change daily). The
    upcoming route is polled like Today's tasks (`JOB_INTERVALS.tasks`, a minute) and reloads on a task change.

## Facts from the code

- Home (web components/workspace/workspace.tsx `Home`) builds Today's rows; the "week" need row comes from
  `useBrandData(base, "content", pickWeek)` (`isThisWeek`, lib/calendar-filter.ts). `TodayTasks`
  (components/tasks/today-tasks.tsx) polls `tasks?view=today` with `useJob` and `useTasksChanged`.
- Task visibility: server/tasks/access.ts `visibleTaskSql(v)`; open statuses `openTaskSql`; brand-local day
  `brandLocalDay(now)` and `localToday` in server/tasks/list.ts; rows → DTOs with `rowsFor` + `toTaskDtos` +
  `brandOwners(scope, { key: "tasks.read", includeClients: true })`.
- Projects: server/tasks/projects.ts `projectWhere(v)` (same brand; client → `clientVisible`); milestone
  index `(workProjectId, dueOn)`; project index `(brandId, status, dueOn)`.
- Ads: `GET ads/summary?from&to` (ads.read) → `AdsSummaryDto.totals[]` per currency (`spend`, `roas`). Sales:
  `GET sales/summary?from&to` (crm.read) → `SalesSummaryDto.currencies[]` (`netExVat`, `exVatFrom`, `exVatMissing`,
  `totals.orders`). Sources: `GET data-sources` (brand.read) → `sources[]` (`kind` META_ADS / SHOPIFY_ORDERS) and
  `timeZone`. Web helpers: lib/data-api.ts (`presetEnd`, `presetRange`, `zoneToday`, `rangeQuery`, `brandApi`),
  components/data/shared.tsx `money`.
- The Overview page (app/(shell)/page.tsx) passes `tasksOn`/`digestOn` from `featureOn`; the Performance page shows
  the Sales tab for `sales` + crm.read and Ads for `ads` + ads.read.
- Design (docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §4): inside a panel one inset `px-5`, sections separated by spacing, not dividers;
  Today's rows are `rounded-xl` boxes inside the panel card. New sections follow Today's section layout.

## Tasks

### Task 1: server `GET brands/:b/upcoming`

Files: `server/tasks/upcoming.ts` (new), `app/api/v1/workspaces/[workspaceId]/brands/[brandId]/upcoming/route.ts`
(new), `shared/api/upcoming.ts` (new: `UpcomingDto`, `UpcomingMilestoneDto`; its own file, not `shared/api/tasks.ts`,
which the tasks-calendar stream also edits) + one export line in `shared/index.ts`, `server/tasks/list.ts` (export
`localToday`, `rowsFor`, `DUE`, additive), `server/tasks/projects.ts` (export `projectWhere`, additive),
`tests/db/upcoming.db.test.ts` (new).

- `upcomingWork(scope, v, now)` in `server/tasks/upcoming.ts`, reusing the exported helpers (no second copy):
  - Tasks: one raw query, filter on `DUE` (`COALESCE(t."dueOn", 'infinity')`, the same expression as the (brand, due,
    id) index and list.ts's views) `BETWEEN localToday AND localToday + 6`, `'WEBSITE'::"TaskArea" = ANY(t.areas)`,
    `openTaskSql`, `visibleTaskSql(v)`, order `DUE, id`, limit 10, total via `count(*) OVER ()`; rows → `rowsFor` →
    `toTaskDtos`.
  - Milestones: one raw query in parallel with the tasks (no extra sequential round trip to the remote DB), joined to
    `WorkProject` with the same rule as `projectWhere` (workspace, brand, `clientVisible` for a client) and
    `status = 'ACTIVE'`, `doneAt IS NULL`, `dueOn BETWEEN localToday AND localToday + 6`, order `dueOn, id`, limit 10,
    `count(*) OVER ()`. The project rule is written once as SQL beside `projectWhere` (`projectVisibleSql(v)`) with a
    test that both agree.
  - The window's days come back from the same SQL (`localToday`), so `from`/`to` in the answer are the brand's days.
  - Returns `{ from, to, website: { count, tasks }, milestones: { count, items: [{ id, title, dueOn, project: { id,
    name } }] } }`.
- Route: `authorizeBrandRoute(..., "tasks.read")`, `taskViewer(scope)`, `Cache-Control: private, no-store`,
  `taskProblem ?? fromAccessError`.
- Tests (DB): window edges in a Paris brand (today, today + 6 in, today + 7 out, yesterday out); non-WEBSITE and DONE
  tasks out; a client sees only a shared task and only milestones of a clientVisible project; ARCHIVED project and done
  milestone out; another brand's rows out; counts beyond the cap.
- `npm run sync:shared` from the server worktree: web and mobile worktrees (`sma-overview/{web,mobile}`, branch
  `overview-gaps`) sit side by side, so all three `.hash` files match; commit the copies in web and mobile.

### Task 2: web "Next 7 days" section

Files: `components/workspace/upcoming-week.tsx` (new), `components/workspace/workspace.tsx` (Home), a pure helper
`lib/upcoming.ts` (merge + sort + cap) with `tests/upcoming.test.ts`.

- `UpcomingWeek({ base, tasksOn, onOpen })`: posts from `useBrandData(base, "content", pickWeekPosts)` (stable pick:
  `isThisWeek`, sorted by `scheduledFor`, first 10, plus the count); `useJob(`${base}/upcoming`)` only when `tasksOn`
  (tasks.read), with `useTasksChanged(reload)`. **One sort key:** every row is keyed by a brand-local day
  ("YYYY-MM-DD"): tasks and milestones by `dueOn`, a post by its `scheduledFor` converted to a day in the brand's zone
  (the route returns the zone's `from`; the post's day is taken with `Intl` in the browser's zone when the route isn't
  loaded). Ties: posts by instant, then kind (post, website, milestone), then title. Tested at the day boundary.
  Rows merged by date (`mergeUpcoming`), each with a kind label (Post +
  channel, Website, Milestone · project), date label and a button that opens the post peek, the task peek or links to
  the project. "N more" links: calendar `?cf_need=week`, `/tasks?area=website`, `/projects`.
- Home: drop the `week` need row and its `weekQ`; render `<UpcomingWeek>` as a section under Today.
- Tests: `mergeUpcoming` ordering (date, then kind), cap and "more" counts; empty input.

### Task 3: web Performance summary

Files: `components/workspace/performance-summary.tsx` (new), `components/workspace/workspace.tsx` (prop + Home),
`app/(shell)/page.tsx` (`adsOn`, `salesOn` from `featureOn`), a pure helper in the same file or
`lib/performance-summary.ts` with `tests/performance-summary.test.ts`.

- Shown when `adsOn && ads.read` or `salesOn && crm.read`; otherwise nothing (no heading).
- Loads `data-sources`, then in parallel only the summaries the person may read, with the 28-day range from the
  sources' `timeZone`. One `useLoad`; LoadFailed with Retry on error; skeleton while loading.
- Tiles (links): Ad spend, ROAS (per currency), Net sales excl. VAT, Orders (per currency). Each is a `Link` to
  its tab with the range, built on `Stat` and `money` (components/data/shared.tsx). The Excl. VAT notes come from the
  Sales tab's own logic, exported from sales-tab.tsx (`exVatNotes(c)`), not a second copy. Footer line: "Last 28 days
  · <range>".
- **Source states, honest (Decision 9/10 plus):** no source → "not connected / not turned on"; a source with no
  successful sync yet (`lastResult?.okAt` null) → `syncStatus(...)` text ("Not synced yet", "Syncing…", "Sync
  queued…") and **no numbers**; a source whose last run failed (`status === "ERROR"`) → numbers from the last good
  sync with "Last sync failed" and a link to the tab; otherwise numbers with "Updated 3 h ago" (`syncStatus`, from
  components/data/synced-at.tsx). "No ad spend / No orders in these 28 days" only after a successful sync.
- Tests: the pure `adsView` / `salesView` helpers that turn (sources, summary) into "not connected" / "empty" /
  tiles, including `netExVat: null` → "—" with its reason, and several currencies.

## Ceilings

- Customer follow-ups: nothing to show until a stored inbox or follow-up queue exists (features 25, 26).
- Posts in the list come from the content list (calendar window); a brand with more than the list's row cap of open
  posts could miss some, the same as the existing count.
- The upcoming route runs two queries in parallel per minute per open Overview (10 + 10 rows, window counts). Tasks
  filter on the same `COALESCE(dueOn, 'infinity')` expression as the (brand, due, id) index; the area and visibility
  filters run on the index's window. Milestones go project-first (`(brandId, status, dueOn)`) then
  `(workProjectId, dueOn)`; fine to thousands of projects. Past that, an index on WorkProjectMilestone `(dueOn)` with
  a brand column would be the upgrade.
- Performance numbers are read once per Overview visit; a sync finishing while the page is open shows on the next visit.

## Review changes

Four read-only lens reviewers (correctness, security, performance, maintainability) read the plan against the code.
No Critical finding stood after the fixes below; all Important ones are folded in. These override the task text above
where they differ.

**Server (Task 1)**
- **Window from the brand's zone in JS, then everything in parallel** (performance 3, maintainability 1, correctness
  1 and 9). `upcomingWork` reads the zone once with the existing `brandTimeZone(scope)` (server/data/sources.ts) and
  takes `from = zoneToday(zone, now)` (same file), `to = from + 6`. Then four reads run in parallel: task ids (limit
  10), task count, milestones (Prisma `findMany` with `workProject: { ...projectWhere(v), status: "ACTIVE" }`, take
  10, project name), milestone count. Counts are their own `count(*)` queries, so an empty window answers 0. No
  `projectVisibleSql` copy, no `localToday` export; `projectWhere` and `rowsFor` are exported (one word each), `DUE`
  moves to server/tasks/sql.ts beside `openTaskSql` (list.ts keeps an import). The answer carries `timeZone`, `from`
  and `to`.
- **`toTaskDtos(scope, rows)` without `people`** (performance 2): an empty window skips the two people queries.
- **Visibility tests widened** (security 2): staff without tasks.all sees only their own website task; a WEBSITE+CRM
  task is hidden without a CRM key; a client reviewer sees only shared tasks and milestones of client-visible projects;
  counts follow the same filter; another brand's rows and archived projects are out; empty window counts 0.
- **Facts fixed** (correctness 5, maintainability 2): `brandLocalDay`/`openTaskSql` live in server/tasks/sql.ts.

**Web (Tasks 2 and 3)**
- **Gate = flag and permission** (security 1, 4): Home passes `tasksOn && tasks.read` to UpcomingWeek; the
  performance block fetches `ads/summary` only with `adsOn && ads.read` and `sales/summary` only with `salesOn &&
  crm.read`, never fetches to hide, and never shows `shopRevenueOverSpend`.
- **Stable fetchers** (performance 1, 6): the summary fetcher is a `useCallback` keyed on `base` and the two gates
  (Home re-renders every few seconds); `pickWeekPosts` is module level; the merge is memoised.
- **One default range** (maintainability 3): `defaultRange(timeZone)` added to lib/data-api.ts and used by
  performance-tabs.tsx and the summary; links built with `withRange`-style params from computed dates.
- **Shared wording** (maintainability 4): `exVatNotes(c)` extracted from `ExVatHeadline` (sales-tab.tsx) and used by
  both; the Ads tab's "In the ad account's currency" text reused as an exported constant. `netExVat: null` shows "—"
  with "Excl. VAT not available for these days yet" (the tab shows a bare "—"; the Overview adds the few words).
- **Honest states, completed** (correctness 2, 3, 4, 8): sources with `status === "OFF"` count as not connected;
  "Last sync failed" when `lastResult.reason` is set (ERROR is "reconnect needed"); several Meta accounts: numbers when
  any account has synced, "N accounts not synced yet" otherwise; a currency with `orders === 0` (only in the previous
  period) is dropped, and "No orders" means none left; a currency with spend 0 is dropped; null ROAS is "—".
- **Posts on brand days** (correctness 1, 6): a post's day is `zoneToday(timeZone, scheduledFor)` with the route's
  zone; posts past `to` (the 168 h window can reach an eighth day) are left out of the list, so all rows sit in the
  same 7 days. Without the route (tasks off), posts sort by instant. Decision 3 stands otherwise.
- **Known overlaps, kept on purpose** (correctness 7, 10): your own website task due today shows in My tasks and in
  Next 7 days (the second is the week's view of the site, not your to-do). With the week row gone, Today may say
  "Nothing to do today" while posts are coming up; the Next 7 days section sits right below.
- **Pure helpers in lib/** (maintainability 7): `lib/upcoming.ts` (`mergeUpcoming`) and `lib/performance-summary.ts`
  (`adsView`, `salesView`), tested in node.
- Week row removal lists every piece: `pickWeek`, `weekQ`, `needs.week`, the tuple entry (maintainability 5).
- **Merge hygiene** (maintainability 6): after rebasing on main, re-run `npm run sync:shared`; never hand-merge `.hash`;
  the new export goes at the end of shared/index.ts.

**Not taken:** a `?lite=1` on ads/summary (performance 5): one read per Overview visit, not worth a new parameter.
Data-sources before the summaries is two calls in a row (performance 7), accepted for a one-time read.

## Branch review (2026-11-27)

Four read-only lens reviewers on the finished branch. Security: no findings. Fixed:
- **Performance 1:** the route sends website tasks as `{ id, title, dueOn }` (no `toTaskDtos`, which cost several
  sequential round trips on a minute poll); `rowsFor` is private again.
- **Correctness 1:** the ads and sales summaries settle separately (`Promise.allSettled`); a failed one shows Retry in
  its own block, the other keeps its figures.
- **Correctness 2:** posts keep the calendar's 7 × 24 h window (an eighth brand day can show), matching the filter the
  section links to. **Correctness 3:** with tasks on, rows wait for the route's zone so they don't move once it lands.
- **Correctness 4:** an unknown stored zone falls back to UTC instead of a 500 (DB test).
- **Performance 4/5, maintainability 6:** the section's own focus listener is gone (useJob refetches on return); a task
  change reloads the route only for someone who reads tasks.
- **Maintainability 1, 3, 4, 5, 7:** `addDays` reused from server/data/sources.ts; `exVatNotes`, `roas`,
  `ADS_CURRENCY_NOTE` and `isSynced` live in lib/performance-summary.ts and the tabs import them; `addDays` from
  lib/data-api for "Tomorrow". **Maintainability 2:** `defaultRange`'s comment says what it mirrors (the tab keeps its
  own `end`, which its date filter needs).

Not taken: computing the window in SQL to save the zone read (performance 2) and window counts (performance 3), minor
at one poll a minute; reload past midnight (correctness 5, accepted by Decision 13); refunds-only currencies
(correctness 6, low impact, in HANDOVER open items); `DUE` alias in list.ts kept to keep that file's diff small.
