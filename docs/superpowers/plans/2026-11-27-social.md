> plan before Task 1 and on the branch before merge; see "Review changes" at the end.

**Goal:** Clicking Social & Content (Wave's slot in the sidebar) opens a list of channels: Meta, Instagram,
Pinterest, LinkedIn, X, Threads and Blog, each with its connection status, its next scheduled item and how many posts
wait for approval. Clicking a channel opens the Content calendar filtered to it. The calendar's channel filter gains
two groups: **Meta** (Facebook and every Instagram format) and **Instagram** (the four Instagram formats), so the Meta
calendar and the Instagram shortcut show the same posts.

**Architecture:** one small read-only server route, `GET content/summary` (per channel: posts awaiting approval and
the next dated post), plus the existing `GET connections`; the rest is web. No migration, no `shared/` change (the route
uses `OPEN_STATUSES` / `STATUS_GROUPS` from `shared/`), no mobile change. (The first draft reused `GET content`; the
plan review showed why not: see Review changes.)

**Tech stack:** Next.js app router page (server component gate + client component), lucide icons, vitest.

**Estimate:** 2 dev days (T0 0.5, T1 0.25, T2 0.25, T3 0.5, T4 0.5), plus the branch review.

## Decisions (my calls; the owner may override any)

1. **A small summary endpoint, `GET content/summary` (`brand.read`).** `GET content` caps at 200 rows with undated
   posts sorted first, so a brand with many undated planner posts would lose every dated post from the landing, and it
   signs photo URLs the landing never shows. The summary answers
   `{ channels: [{ channel, awaiting, next: { id, title, scheduledFor, status } | null }] }` for every channel enum
   value, from one `groupBy` and one `findFirst` per channel (9, in parallel, on the `(brandId, scheduledFor)` index).
   "Awaiting" and "open" come from `STATUS_GROUPS.awaiting` / `OPEN_STATUSES` in `shared/`, the calendar's own words.
   The web sums a group's channels.
2. **Route `/social`, titled "Social & Content".** A NAV entry of group `"page"` (titled, listed nowhere), as Projects
   and Competitors are. Wave's slot points at `/social` and its role line reads **"Social & Content"** (was "Content
   calendar"); the agent-based sidebar stays as it is. Wave stays selected on both `/social` and `/calendar`. More's
   "Content calendar" entry still opens the calendar directly.
3. **Rows and what they open** (`cf_channel` value):

   | Row | Posts counted | Connection shown | Opens |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Meta | Facebook + the 4 Instagram formats | Facebook Page (META), Instagram (INSTAGRAM) | `/calendar?cf_channel=meta` |
   | Instagram | Instagram post, carousel, Reel, story | Instagram | `/calendar?cf_channel=instagram` |
   | Pinterest | Pinterest pin | Pinterest | `/calendar?cf_channel=PINTEREST_PIN` |
   | LinkedIn | LinkedIn post | none: "Posted by hand" | `/calendar?cf_channel=LINKEDIN_POST` |
   | X | X post | none: "Posted by hand" | `/calendar?cf_channel=X_POST` |
   | Threads | Threads post | Threads | `/calendar?cf_channel=THREADS_POST` |
   | Blog | (not calendar posts) | not shown | `/blog` |

   Facebook has no row of its own: the feature list names "Meta: Facebook and Instagram calendar with a platform
   filter", and the Meta calendar's Channel filter picks Facebook alone. **Threads is shown** although the list
   doesn't name it: it is a channel the calendar holds and publishes to, and without a row its posts can't be reached
   from the landing.
4. **Connection status per provider:** no connection → "Not connected"; every connection ACTIVE → "Connected"; any
   ERROR or REVOKED → "Needs reconnecting" (worst wins when a provider has several accounts). Shown as a quiet
   `StatusBadge` with a tone (good / warn / muted) plus the words. LinkedIn and X: "Posted by hand" (muted), they have
   no publishing connection. A role without `CONNECTIONS_READ` (a client reviewer) doesn't call `GET connections`
   (it would 403) and sees no connection column; the counts still show. A failed connections read shows
   "Connection unavailable" on the rows, never "Not connected".
5. **Next scheduled item:** the earliest post on the row's channels with a date from now on and an open status other
   than BLOCKED (`OPEN_STATUSES` minus BLOCKED: a blocked post can't go out; published, rejected and cancelled posts
   don't count). It is the next *planned* post, so a dated draft counts; its status is shown beside it as a quiet
   badge, so "Draft in review" and "Scheduled" read differently. Shown as its title and date/time, linking to the
   calendar with that post open (`?cf_channel=…&post=<id>`). None → "Nothing scheduled".
6. **Pending approvals:** posts on the row's channels with status `AWAITING_APPROVAL` (`STATUS_GROUPS.awaiting`, the
   calendar's "Awaiting approval" status filter). Non-zero: "N awaiting approval", linking to the calendar with
   `cf_status=awaiting` too. Zero: "None waiting" (muted).
7. **Blog row:** shown only while the `blog` flag is on for the workspace (the page already requires `brand.read`,
   which `/blog` needs too) (production has it off; a row that opens a
   locked page helps nobody). It links to `/blog`. Blog articles have no publish date and aren't calendar posts, so
   next item and approvals show an em dash with "Articles live on the Blog page" (design system: never 0 for missing
   data). Counting articles in review is left for the blog-calendar work (item 9).
8. **Calendar channel groups:** `cf_channel` accepts `meta` and `instagram` beside the channel enum values. The
   Channel filter lists the two groups first ("Meta: Facebook + Instagram", "Instagram: all formats"), then the
   channels present, and **always includes the current value** (a group, or a channel with no posts yet, e.g. LinkedIn
   opened from the landing), so the pill never reads "All channels" while a filter is on. Picking Facebook in the
   Meta calendar is the "platform filter". `filterFromSearch` accepts only a known channel or group for
   `cf_channel` (as it already does for status, owner and need); anything else is dropped.
9. **Channel icons** move out of content-panels.tsx into `lib/post-labels.ts` beside `CHANNEL_LABEL` (the landing
   must not load the 1,500-line panels module); the two group labels and icons live there too.
10. **The landing polls the summary every 30 s** (`JOB_INTERVALS.socialLanding`; a passive overview needs no 6 s
    freshness; `useJob` already pauses in a hidden tab). Connections are read on mount. The page remounts the landing
    on a brand switch (`key={activeBrandId}`), as the calendar page does.
11. **Connection wording and tone** are the Connections tab's: ACTIVE -> good "Connected", otherwise bad "Needs
    reconnect" (one `connectionBadge(status)` in `lib/status.ts`, used by both). None -> muted "Not connected".
12. **One `calendarHref(filter, post?)`** in `lib/calendar-filter.ts`; the Overview's private one becomes a call to it.

## Ceilings

- The summary is 10 small queries per poll (every 30 s per open landing). No channel index: `findFirst` walks the
  brand's future dated posts in date order; fine at hundreds per brand. Upgrade: a `(brandId, channel, scheduledFor)`
  index, or one `DISTINCT ON (channel)` raw query.
- The calendar a row opens still reads the 200-row window, so with 200+ undated open posts a channel's next post may
  show on the landing but not in the calendar's List until it is in Month. That is the calendar's existing ceiling.
